Post Streak helps you publish consistently without obsessing over views, likes, or follower counts.

Choose a weekly posting target, log each post in two taps, and watch your streak grow. A 365-day heatmap makes your consistency visible at a glance, while recent-post history lets you correct an accidental log.

LOG FROM ANYWHERE

- Log Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or other posts in two taps
- Share a post link to the Post Streak Share Extension
- Delete an accidental log at any time

BUILD A REAL HABIT

- Set and edit your weekly target
- Track this week’s progress
- See current and longest weekly streaks
- Review a full year of publishing activity

OPTIONAL FRIEND ACCOUNTABILITY

- Connect privately using a friend code
- See accepted friends’ newest posts
- Report content or block a user from every feed item
- Your email and personal streak target stay private

Post Streak has no ads, analytics, subscriptions, or engagement metrics. Account deletion is available directly in Settings.
